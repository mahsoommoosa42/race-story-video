## Editorial lessons supported by the project history

- Repeated feedback favoured more approach and real-time exit footage. The initial short montage did not leave enough breathing room. No arbitrary short-form duration target should override comprehension.
- Full widescreen footage preserves cars on either side. Do not centre-crop onboard footage to fill portrait.
- Name and the dramatic title were removed. Black/red/white was explicitly selected after other colour treatments.
- A straight-on chicane incident was ultimately excluded; this is a choice for this race, not a rule to hide mistakes in future edits.
- The Liva pass description was corrected to holding the line and passing down the inside. A better corner exit was not the explanation. This illustrates why descriptive captions must not invent causation.
- The finish wording became only "To the chequered flag." before the result, and the final card retained grid and finish without a low-point statistic.
- Finishing footage continues naturally; no end freeze is needed.
