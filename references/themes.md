# Visual themes

All themes share full-width landscape footage within a portrait canvas, patient race storytelling, delayed outcome captions and the reference's general spacing. These are designed alternatives, not claims that five videos have already been rendered or approved.

| Theme | Character | Best fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trackside Red | Crisp black, red and white; the original reference | Race results and direct competition recaps |
| Heritage Ivory | Warm paper, burgundy, editorial serif headlines | Classic cars, endurance stories, reflective recaps |
| Night Telemetry | Deep navy, cool white, cyan; precise technical labels | Sim racing, night events, modern machinery |
| British Racing | Forest green, cream and gold; restrained club-racing aesthetic | Track days, touring cars, club motorsport |
| Monochrome Journal | Off-white, charcoal and grey; photographic restraint | Documentary race diaries and minimal edits |

## Colour tokens

Use these consistently across title cards, progress indicators and result cards. Primary and secondary text sit on the canvas, never directly over moving footage. Accent is a foreground colour on the canvas; if using a filled accent badge, use the canvas colour for its text.

For custom overrides, check normal text contrast against its background (at least 4.5:1). Do not rely on colour alone to show chapter progress: retain labels, segment structure and a distinct current marker. Dividers may be subtle because they are decorative.

## Typography and graphics

- **Trackside Red:** Bold sans-serif captions and result; monospace uppercase chapter labels. Thin segmented progress rule fills red through the current chapter. Match the packaged reference closely.
- **Heritage Ivory:** Bold editorial serif captions and result, with plain sans-serif supporting text and small uppercase labels. Fine burgundy chapter rules and a numbered chapter marker. No fake paper texture over the footage.
- **Night Telemetry:** Squared or condensed sans-serif headlines with monospace labels and numerals. Cyan current-chapter marker and fine navy-grey rules. Technical styling is decorative: do not invent speed, lap time, telemetry or data readouts.
- **British Racing:** Bold humanist sans-serif captions, serif result numeral if available, small widely spaced sans-serif chapter labels. Gold progress segments and a fine cream result divider. Avoid invented team badges or manufacturer branding.
- **Monochrome Journal:** Bold sans-serif captions with regular serif supporting text, compact sans-serif chapter labels. Dark completed segments, outlined upcoming segments and a labelled current chapter. Use strong size contrast for the finish result rather than a new colour.

Use installed fonts that match these roles; do not silently download proprietary fonts. Preserve hierarchy if the exact family is unavailable. Keep main captions short and wrapping within the reference margins; compare the longest caption and result card at phone size before rendering the whole timeline.

Theme changes affect surrounding graphics by default, not source colour grading, playback speed, transitions, soundtrack or story. Apply grading only when requested or needed to correct visibly inconsistent footage, and preserve useful visibility of cars and track markings.
